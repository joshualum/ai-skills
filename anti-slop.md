# Anti-Slop Writing Skill

When generating UI copy, microcopy, or product documentation, strictly adhere to these rules:
- **Banned Phrases:** Never use "seamless", "game-changer", "effortless", "unlock", "dive deep", or "it's worth noting".
- **Tone:** Direct, concise, active voice.
- **Microcopy:** Keep button labels and headings under 6 words.
